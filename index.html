WHAT IT HANDLES

Eight things Trakit does for you

Record sales

Log every sale as it happens — item, price, quantity — instead of trying to remember it by closing time.

Add product

Keep your full product list in one place, with prices and quantities ready whenever you restock or sell.

Add supplier

Store who you buy from — contact details and what they supply — so reordering is one lookup, not a search through old messages.

Purchase list

Track what you've bought in for the shop, so cost of goods is never a guess at month end.

Debt tracking

See who owes you, how much, and since when — no more chasing customers based on memory alone.

Analytics

See which products move, which don't, and how the shop is doing week over week, laid out plainly.

Create invoice
